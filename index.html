Apex Training

A calendar-first training planner, live workout tracker, and AI coach in one app. Plan your week with recurring workouts, log sets against planned targets, and track personal records computed deterministically from your own history — the AI narrates your data, it never invents it. Includes a standards-compliant ICS feed for subscribing from Apple or Google Calendar.

Apex is a personal project by Shane Haynes and is currently invite-only. Source and specs: github.com/shanehaynes/apex-training.
