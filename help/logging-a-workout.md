# Logging a workout

Log each set as you do it. This page covers the two buttons on a workout and the grey numbers. It also covers blank sets and the trophies at the end.

## 1. Start Workout or Mark as Complete

![The Start Workout and Mark as Complete buttons on a workout](/help/logging-a-workout/01-start-or-mark.phone.png)

Tap a workout on your calendar to open it. You will see two buttons.

**Start Workout** opens the tracker. Use it when you want to write down each set.

**Mark as Complete** ticks the workout off without logging any sets. Use it when you only want to say "done." It then says **Completed**. Tap **Completed** to undo.

## 2. The grey numbers are last time

![Grey numbers in the boxes: the weight and reps from last time](/help/logging-a-workout/02-grey-numbers.phone.png)

Each set has a box for each thing to log, like **weight** and **reps**. The target for the set is on the left.

If you did this exercise before, the boxes show last time's numbers in grey. Grey numbers are a hint. They are not saved yet.

## 3. Keep them or type over them

![Set 1 now reads 135 and 8 in white; sets 2 and 3 are still grey](/help/logging-a-workout/03-one-set-typed.phone.png)

Tap a grey number to keep that whole set. The numbers turn white, which means they are saved.

To change a number, tap it and type. Here set 1 went up to 135. Sets 2 and 3 are still grey.

Everything saves as you type. There is no save button.

## 4. Finish, and blank sets

![The bar that says 8 planned sets unlogged, with Keep going and Finish anyway](/help/logging-a-workout/04-unlogged-bar.phone.png)

Tap **Finish** at the top when you are done.

If some sets are still blank or grey, a bar tells you how many. Tap **Keep going** to go back and fill them in. Tap **Finish anyway** to finish now.

Blank sets are saved as zero. The summary marks them "skipped," so your history stays honest.

## 5. The summary and its trophies

![The Workout Complete summary with two trophies](/help/logging-a-workout/05-summary-trophies.phone.png)

After **Finish**, you see **Workout Complete**. It shows a note from your coach and every set you logged.

A trophy means a personal record: you beat your best on that exercise. The line says what you did and the best it beat.

**est. best single lift** comes from the weight and reps of your best set.

Your first time on an exercise never earns a trophy. There is nothing to beat yet.

Tap **Back to calendar** to leave. Tap the **X** to stay on the tracker instead.

## 6. On a computer

![The tracker on a computer, with Finish at the top right](/help/logging-a-workout/06-tracker.desktop.png)

The tracker works the same on a computer. **Finish** is at the top right.

## More you can do

- **Add set** under an exercise adds one more set. Tap the **X** on an added set to remove it.
- The two-arrow button next to an exercise's name swaps it. Pick another exercise to log instead. This changes today only, and your plan stays the same.
- The back arrow at the top left takes you to your calendar. Your sets are kept, so you can come back.
- **Cancel workout** at the bottom deletes everything you logged for this session.

## Editing a finished workout

Open the workout and tap **View / Edit Workout**. Change any number. Edits save as you type.

Tap **Done** at the top to see the summary again.
