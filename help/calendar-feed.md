# See your workouts in your phone’s calendar

Already live in Apple Calendar or Google Calendar? Apex can put your workouts there too, next to everything else. You copy one address from Apex and paste it into your calendar once. After that, new and changed workouts show up on their own.

## 1. Copy your calendar address

In Apex, tap your picture at the top left to open **Profile**. Scroll down to **Connections** and tap **Calendar feed** to open it.

![Calendar feed open, with the copy button next to the address](/help/calendar-feed/01-feed-address.phone.png)

Tap the copy button, the one with two small squares. Apex says **Address copied**. The address is now ready to paste.

## 2. On an iPhone: add it to Apple Calendar

Open the **Calendar** app. Tap **Calendars** at the bottom, then **Add Calendar**, then **Add Subscription Calendar**.

<!-- EXTERNAL: iOS Calendar app (iPhone) — Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar, the Subscription URL field filled with a feed address, Subscribe button visible; 375 wide; redact the URL -->
![The Subscription URL box, with the Apex address pasted in](/help/calendar-feed/02-ios-add-subscription.phone.png)

Touch and hold the **Subscription URL** box, then tap **Paste**. Tap **Subscribe**, then **Add**.

## 3. On Google Calendar: add it on a computer

Google’s phone app cannot add a calendar from an address. This step needs a computer. Go to calendar.google.com and sign in.

On the left, find **Other calendars**. Click the **+** next to it, then **From URL**.

<!-- EXTERNAL: https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r/settings/addbyurl — Settings → Add calendar → From URL, the URL of calendar field filled with a feed address, Add calendar button visible; 1280 wide; redact the URL and the account name -->
![The From URL page, with the Apex address pasted in](/help/calendar-feed/03-google-from-url.desktop.png)

Paste the address into **URL of calendar**. Leave **Make the calendar publicly accessible** unticked. Click **Add calendar**. Your phone’s Google Calendar app picks it up by itself.

## 4. Look for your workouts

Open your calendar and go to a day with a workout on it. It shows up as its own calendar, called **Apex Training**.

<!-- EXTERNAL: iOS Calendar app (iPhone) — day view on a day with an Apex workout from the subscribed Apex Training calendar; 375 wide; redact every other event -->
![A day in the phone calendar, with an Apex workout on it](/help/calendar-feed/04-phone-calendar-result.phone.png)

## Good to know

- **It only shows.** The calendar reads your workouts from Apex. Moving or deleting one in your calendar changes nothing in Apex. Make changes in Apex.
- **Changes take a few hours.** Your calendar checks for updates on its own schedule. Google can take up to a day.
- **Keep the address private.** Anyone with it can read your schedule. Do not post it or send it to anyone.
