# Connect your COROS watch

Link your watch once, and your runs, rides and hikes come in on their own. You never type them in. This page shows the one-time setup and what happens after.

## 1. Open Profile and find COROS

Tap your picture at the top left to open **Profile**. Under **Connections**, tap **COROS** to open it. Then tap **Connect COROS**.

![The COROS section in Profile, with the Connect COROS button](/help/connect-coros/01-connect-coros.phone.png)

No COROS section in Profile? Then this copy of Apex does not offer watch linking yet.

## 2. Sign in to COROS

Apex sends you to the COROS website. Sign in with the email and password you use in the COROS app.

<!-- EXTERNAL: https://mcpus.coros.com/oauth2/authorize — COROS sign-in page reached from Connect COROS, email and password fields empty or email typed; 375 wide; redact email -->
![The COROS sign-in page](/help/connect-coros/02-coros-sign-in.phone.png)

Apex never sees your COROS password. You type it on the COROS site, not in Apex.

## 3. Let Apex read your activities

COROS asks if Apex may read your activities. Check that it names Apex, then allow it.

<!-- EXTERNAL: https://mcpus.coros.com/oauth2/authorize — COROS consent screen after sign-in, naming Apex and the access it asks for, allow button visible; 375 wide; redact nothing -->
![COROS asking you to allow Apex](/help/connect-coros/03-coros-consent.phone.png)

COROS then sends you back to Apex. A message says **COROS connected**.

## 4. Bring in your activities with Sync

Tap **Sync** at the top of the calendar. Your recent activities come in, with the date and time you did them.

![The Sync button at the top of the calendar on a phone](/help/connect-coros/04-sync-button.phone.png)

On a phone, **Sync** is the circling arrows at the top. On a computer, the button also says **Sync**.

![The Sync button at the top of the calendar on a computer](/help/connect-coros/04-sync-button.desktop.png)

You can tap **Sync** any time. Tapping it twice never makes copies.

## 5. Fill it or Keep separate

Sometimes a watch activity looks like a workout you planned for that day. Apex does not guess. It asks you, one activity at a time.

![A card asking to fill a planned workout, with Keep separate and Fill it buttons](/help/connect-coros/05-fill-or-keep.phone.png)

- **Fill it** puts the watch numbers into the workout you planned. It is marked done.
- **Keep separate** adds the activity as its own workout. The planned one stays as it was.

Either way, your plan stays as you wrote it. Activities that match nothing come in on their own, with no question.

## 6. See your heart rate and route

Open a workout that came from your watch. It shows **Synced from COROS**, your heart rate, distance, climb and calories.

![A run from the watch, with heart rate, distance and a heart-rate chart](/help/connect-coros/06-synced-run.phone.png)

Below are charts of your heart rate and elevation, and your route's shape. Apex draws them itself, so your location never goes to a map company.

## 7. Let it happen every night

Back in **Profile → COROS**, the box **Bring in new activities every night** is ticked. Each night around 11:30 PM Eastern, Apex brings in new activities for you.

![COROS connected, with the nightly checkbox ticked and the Disconnect COROS button](/help/connect-coros/07-connected.phone.png)

Activities that match a planned workout still wait for your yes. When some are waiting, the **Sync** button shows a number. Tap it to see them.

Want to bring them in only by hand? Untick the box.

## If it says Reconnect

Now and then the link to COROS runs out. The **Sync** button changes to **Reconnect**, and Profile says **Reconnect needed**.

Tap **Reconnect**, then **Reconnect COROS**, and sign in to COROS again. On a phone, **Reconnect** is the circling arrows at the top. Nothing you already brought in is lost.

## To unlink your watch

Tap **Disconnect COROS** in **Profile → COROS**. Everything already in your calendar stays.

COROS still lists Apex as allowed until you remove it there too. In the COROS app, go to **Profile → Settings → 3rd Party Apps** and remove Apex.
